# BrickUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 (version 1.1)

BrickUp works without an account, a server or advertising. The developer receives no personal data. Push-up counting uses one third-party component, Google MediaPipe, which sends usage statistics to Google (see below). Everything else happens on your iPhone or in your own Apple services.

## Data we collect
The developer collects nothing: BrickUp does not receive, store, sell or share any personal data, and has no accounts, no advertising identifiers and no tracking.

**Google MediaPipe usage statistics.** Counting with the camera uses Google MediaPipe Pose Landmarker, which runs on your iPhone. Google states that MediaPipe Tasks "send metrics about the performance and utilization of the APIs" to Google, which Google uses to measure, debug, maintain and improve MediaPipe ([Google's MediaPipe notice](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/tasks)). Images and video are processed on your iPhone and are not sent. BrickUp does not link these statistics to you and does not use them for tracking. They are sent only while you count with the camera; you are told this on the camera screen before the camera starts. App Store privacy label: **Diagnostics** (performance data, other diagnostic data) — not linked to you, not used for tracking.

## Camera
When you count a set, BrickUp uses the front camera and Google MediaPipe Pose Landmarker, running on your iPhone, to find the positions of your shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips and ankles. Only these positions are used, frame by frame, to count push-ups. Video and images are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The camera is on only while you are counting. Camera permission is asked the first time you count.

## Push-up records
Your sets (date, time, number of push-ups) are stored on your iPhone.

- **Backup**: the records are part of your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), so restoring a new iPhone from that backup brings them back. BrickUp has no server of its own; the developer cannot see this data.
- **Home-screen widget**: the widget shows your brick count and wall height. The app shares only these numbers with the widget through storage on your iPhone (an App Group); nothing leaves the device.

## Apple Health (optional)
Off by default. If you turn on "Log to Apple Health", iOS asks for permission, and each saved set is written to Apple Health as a Functional Strength Training workout (start time, end time and the number of push-ups). BrickUp only writes; it never reads your Health data. You can turn this off in the app or in the Health app at any time. Health data is never used for advertising and never sent to the developer.

## Export
If you choose "Export records", iOS shows the share sheet with a CSV text of your sets. BrickUp does not upload it; you choose where it goes.

## Retention and deletion
Records stay until you delete them. You can delete any set inside the app (Records › Edit). Deleting the app removes the records on this iPhone; older iPhone backups are managed in iOS Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage. Workouts written to Apple Health are managed in the Health app.

## Children
The developer does not collect data from anyone, including children. Google MediaPipe usage statistics are described above.

## Changes
If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.

# BrickUp 개인정보 처리방침 (요약)

- **개발자는 정보를 수집하지 않습니다.** 계정 · 서버 · 광고가 없고, 개발자는 어떤 개인정보도 받지 않습니다.

- **카메라**: 셀 때만 켜지고, iPhone 안에서 구글 MediaPipe로 어깨 · 팔꿈치 · 손목 · 엉덩이 · 발목 위치만 봅니다. 영상과 사진은 저장하거나 보내지 않습니다.

- **내보내기**: 직접 고른 곳으로만 공유 시트를 통해 보냅니다.
- **삭제**: 앱에서 세트를 하나씩 지울 수 있습니다(기록 › 편집). 예전 iPhone 백업은 설정 › Apple 계정 › iCloud › 저장 공간 관리에서 지울 수 있습니다.

# BrickUp プライバシーポリシー（要約）

- **開発者はデータを収集しません。** アカウント・サーバー・広告はなく、開発者は個人データを一切受け取りません。
- **Google MediaPipeの利用統計**：カメラでのカウントに使うGoogle MediaPipeが、機能の性能・利用状況の統計をGoogleに送信します。映像や写真は送信せず、ユーザーとの紐付けやトラッキングには使いません。カメラをオンにする前の画面でお知らせします（App Storeの表示：診断 — ユーザーに関連付けられない）。
- **カメラ**：数えるときだけオンになり、iPhone内のGoogle MediaPipeで肩・ひじ・手首・腰・足首の位置だけを見ます。映像や写真は保存も送信もしません。
- **記録**：iPhoneに保存され、iPhoneのバックアップ（iCloudバックアップ・コンピュータのバックアップ）にも含まれます。ウィジェットにはレンガの数と高さだけをiPhone内で渡します。
- **Appleヘルスケア（任意）**：初期設定はオフ。オンにすると、セットごとに「筋力トレーニング」として書き込むだけで、ヘルスケアのデータは読みません。
- **書き出し**：共有シートで、自分で選んだ相手にだけ送ります。
- **削除**：アプリ内でセットを1つずつ削除できます（記録 › 編集）。
