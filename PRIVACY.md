# PUSH TOTAL Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 (version 1.1)

PUSH TOTAL works without an account, a server, advertising or analytics. Everything below happens on your iPhone or in your own Apple services.

## Data we collect
None. The developer does not receive, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no tracking. App Store privacy label: **Data Not Collected**.

## Camera
When you count a set, PUSH TOTAL uses the front camera and Apple's on-device Vision framework to find the positions of your shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips and ankles. Only these positions are used, frame by frame, to count push-ups. Video and images are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The camera is on only while you are counting. Camera permission is asked the first time you count.

## Push-up records
Your sets (date, time, number of push-ups) are stored on your iPhone.

- **Backup**: the records are part of your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), so restoring a new iPhone from that backup brings them back. PUSH TOTAL has no server of its own; the developer cannot see this data.
- **Home-screen widget**: the widget shows your brick count and wall height. The app shares only these numbers with the widget through storage on your iPhone (an App Group); nothing leaves the device.

## Apple Health (optional)
Off by default. If you turn on "Log to Apple Health", iOS asks for permission, and each saved set is written to Apple Health as a Functional Strength Training workout (start time, end time and the number of push-ups). PUSH TOTAL only writes; it never reads your Health data. You can turn this off in the app or in the Health app at any time. Health data is never used for advertising and never sent to the developer.

## Export
If you choose "Export records", iOS shows the share sheet with a CSV text of your sets. PUSH TOTAL does not upload it; you choose where it goes.

## Retention and deletion
Records stay until you delete them. You can delete any set inside the app (Records › Edit). Deleting the app removes the records on this iPhone; older iPhone backups are managed in iOS Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage. Workouts written to Apple Health are managed in the Health app.

## Children
PUSH TOTAL does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.

# PUSH TOTAL 개인정보 처리방침 (요약)

- **수집하는 정보 없음.** 계정 · 서버 · 광고 · 분석이 없고, 개발자는 어떤 개인정보도 받지 않습니다(App Store 표시: 수집하는 데이터 없음).
- **카메라**: 셀 때만 켜지고, iPhone 안에서 Apple Vision으로 어깨 · 팔꿈치 · 손목 · 엉덩이 · 발목 위치만 봅니다. 영상과 사진은 저장하거나 보내지 않습니다.
- **기록**: iPhone에 저장되고, iPhone 자체 백업(iCloud 백업 · 컴퓨터 백업)에 함께 들어갑니다. 새 iPhone을 그 백업으로 복원하면 이어집니다(앱 서버 없음, 개발자는 볼 수 없음). 위젯에는 벽돌 수와 높이만 iPhone 안에서 넘깁니다.
- **Apple 건강 (선택)**: 기본은 꺼짐. 켜면 세트마다 '근력 운동'으로 쓰기만 하고, 건강 데이터를 읽지 않습니다.
- **내보내기**: 직접 고른 곳으로만 공유 시트를 통해 보냅니다.
- **삭제**: 앱에서 세트를 하나씩 지울 수 있습니다(기록 › 편집). 예전 iPhone 백업은 설정 › Apple 계정 › iCloud › 저장 공간 관리에서 지울 수 있습니다.

# PUSH TOTAL プライバシーポリシー（要約）

- **収集するデータはありません。** アカウント・サーバー・広告・分析はなく、開発者は個人データを一切受け取りません（App Storeの表示：データの収集なし）。
- **カメラ**：数えるときだけオンになり、iPhone内のApple Visionで肩・ひじ・手首・腰・足首の位置だけを見ます。映像や写真は保存も送信もしません。
- **記録**：iPhoneに保存され、iPhoneのバックアップ（iCloudバックアップ・コンピュータのバックアップ）にも含まれます。ウィジェットにはレンガの数と高さだけをiPhone内で渡します。
- **Appleヘルスケア（任意）**：初期設定はオフ。オンにすると、セットごとに「筋力トレーニング」として書き込むだけで、ヘルスケアのデータは読みません。
- **書き出し**：共有シートで、自分で選んだ相手にだけ送ります。
- **削除**：アプリ内でセットを1つずつ削除できます（記録 › 編集）。
